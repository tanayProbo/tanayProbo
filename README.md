### 🧠 About Me

- 🔭 Founder building **[Bysonairy](https://github.com/tanayProbo)** — neuroprosthetics & soft robotics — and **Klexys**, an autonomous web-dev AI startup powered by a custom agentic SLM
- 🎓 Also running **Synaptix School**, a tech education initiative
- 🩻 Working on AI-driven multi-modal myoelectric prosthetic arm systems and neural-blueprint control for soft continuum robots
- 💻 Full-stack developer across Python, PyTorch, React, and Node.js — with a focus on computer vision, EMG signal processing, and embedded systems
- 📍 Based in Indore, India
- ⚡ Fun fact: I'd rather ship a working prototype than a perfect slide deck
